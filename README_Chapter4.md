# Chapter 4 — The Void: Case Study & Reporting Gaps

This chapter combines structured documentation of protest outcomes
with contextual reporting evidence to highlight potential undercounting.

---

## Chart 9 — Sankey (Iran 2022 Protest Outcomes)

Data represent cumulative documented outcomes up to February 21, 2023.

Primary source:
HRANA (Human Rights Activists News Agency)

Secondary confirmations:
- UK Parliament CBP-9679
- HRANA GitHub statistical compilation

Methodology:

- Manual extraction of cumulative totals
- Construction of aggregated node–link structure
- No inference of individual-level transitions
- Visualization reflects structural outcomes only

Due to censorship and communication blackouts,
figures represent documented minimums.
